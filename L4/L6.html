Acids are substances widely used in nature, everyday life, and industry. They can be weak or strong, organic or inorganic. Acids participate in many chemical reactions, for example, in digestion, fertilizer production, and metal refining. However, acids require careful handling, as they can be hazardous to humans. Therefore, when working with them, it is important to observe safety rules and follow instructions precisely. By studying acids, we better understand the properties of substances and learn how to handle chemical compounds correctly and safely.
